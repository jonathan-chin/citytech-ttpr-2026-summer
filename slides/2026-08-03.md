# The Next Few Weeks

- **Every day starts with a standup**: what you did, what you are doing next, what is blocking you
- Your team **must use GitHub Projects and branches** to manage the work
- Your **progress on the project board is your exit ticket** for the day
- Days may include **mini workshops** or **morale boosting games**
- **Hard deadline**: your project must be **done 2 days before the program ends**

# GitHub Projects

**GitHub Projects** is a project management board built into GitHub, right next to your code.

- Track work as **cards** that you move across **columns** as it progresses
- Cards link to your **issues** and **pull requests**, so the board mirrors the real code
- At a minimum, set up these columns:
    - **Core Features**: your required, must-build work
    - **Tiered Learning Goals**: extras to reach if time allows
    - **In Progress**: cards being worked on right now
    - **Waiting for Review**: a PR is open, awaiting a teammate
    - **Done**: reviewed, merged, and finished

Docs: [GitHub Projects documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/issues/planning-and-tracking-with-projects)

---

# Project Cards

Each **card** is one piece of work: a feature, a task, or a bug.

- **Assign** cards to people; several can share a card (pair programming welcome)
- Cards link to a **GitHub issue** and its **pull request**, tying the board to real code
- For each card, open a **new branch off `main`**, just for that card
- Do **not** put all your work on one big branch; use a **small branch per feature or issue**
- When the branch is ready, open a **PR** and connect it to the card through its **issue**

---

# The Workflow

1. **Assign yourself** to a card, or take one already assigned to you
2. Drag it to **In Progress**
3. **Pull a fresh `main`**, then create your branch
4. **Code.** Blocked? Open an **issue**, link it to the card, and tag teammates or an instructor
5. Done? **Open a PR** and drag the card to **Waiting for Review**
6. Ask a teammate to **review**:
    - Good: drag to **Done**, merge the PR, delete the branch, close the issue
    - Not yet: drag back to **In Progress**, make changes, and repeat

The **instructional team monitors** your boards and how cards move across columns.

# Mini Workshops

- Skills we did not cover, but that **at least one team's project needs**
- Kept **short**: about **20 to 30 minutes**
- **Some teams will be required** to attend a given workshop
- Not required? **Opt in**, or move to another room and keep working
- Each attending team needs **at least one member** present; send more, or have the rest keep building elsewhere
- **All materials go in our GitHub repo**, so you have them even if you skip

Take what your project needs, and keep the rest of your team moving.

# Morale Boosters

- **Games** and other activities to **recharge your batteries** and counter fatigue
- A break from the screen, on purpose
- **Participation is mandatory** for all students

Three weeks is a marathon, and burnout is real.

# Code Along: Set Up Your Board

Follow along in your team's repo. Together we will:

1. Create the team's **GitHub repo**
2. Add a **Project** with its columns: Core Features, Tiered Learning Goals, In Progress, Waiting for Review, Done
3. Open an **issue** and add it to the board as a **card**
4. **Assign** the card and drag it to **In Progress**
5. Branch off `main`, make a small change, and open a **PR**
6. Link the PR to the issue (`Closes #1`) so merging **moves the card to Done**

By the end, your board is real and wired to your code.
